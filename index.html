Yes. If you can edit text in Canva and copy and paste a prompt, you have the skills you need. Every step is written in plain language.

Rebranding the finished product can take an afternoon or a weekend. Once you are set up, the 30 day plan asks for one small task a day, usually around 20 minutes.
